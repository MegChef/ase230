## Question 1.7

Explain why learning manual PHP first is valuable before moving to frameworks.

---

**Answer:**

- Laravel builds on raw PHP by providing a clean, modern structure that simplifies development. Understanding manual PHP first helps developers appreciate what Laravel automates and ensures they can customize behavior when needed.

---

## Question 1.8

Some developers argue that frameworks add unnecessary complexity to simple projects. Based on the lecture content, how would you counter this argument?

---

**Answer:**

- Frameworks are beneficial, especially for newcomers. By handling much of the underlying complexity, they make projects easier to manage, more consistent, and faster to develop.
